# Forge — food & lift tracker

A phone web app for calorie tracking (with barcode scanning) and lift tracking, plus a routine generator based on your equipment and training days.

- Barcode lookups: [Open Food Facts](https://world.openfoodfacts.org)
- Generic food search: [USDA FoodData Central](https://fdc.nal.usda.gov)
- All personal data is stored locally on your device (export backups from Settings).

Open the site on your phone and choose **Add to Home Screen** to install it.
